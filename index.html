A site does not become AI-ready once

Your content changes, your theme updates, the engines change. So this runs as a loop, not a project.

  1. Audit3webobs scans your domain across 167 criteria, in three webs. Independent engine, free, no install.
  2. PlanOnly the missing signals that are technical and ours to control. Reviews and backlinks are marked as monitored, never as delivered.
  3. InjectThe signals are published at the Cloudflare Edge from a separate store, on your own hostname. Your site, CMS and theme are untouched.
  4. Re-audit3webobs scores it again. We never mark our own work. You get before and after, both timestamped.
  5. WatchHourly checks. When something drifts — a plugin update, a page change — it is re-injected and you are told.
  6. Run againAny time you want, from the same button. The history stays, so the evolution is provable.

What this does not do

The injector makes your site legible to AI. It does not make it credible. Legibility is installed. Credibility is earned.

  • We do not promise that AI will cite you. Citations are earned through content and external corroboration.
  • We do not promise rankings, traffic, or visibility inside any specific assistant.
  • We do not certify legal compliance. We publish the technical transparency artifacts you sign off on.
  • We audit 167 criteria, of which 140 are directly testable. Far fewer are injectable — the report marks every row honestly.
  • Reviews, backlinks, press and directory profiles are not ours to install. We monitor them and tell you when they move.
