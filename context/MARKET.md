## 1. THE UNCOMFORTABLE TRUTH ABOUT DEMAND

The need is real. The vocabulary is not. Almost no buyer wakes up wanting an
"AI-ready injector". They think about AI in narrow buckets: chatbot, automation,
analytics. Being illegible to AI crawlers is not yet a felt pain, because
Google traffic still arrives and nobody audits what ChatGPT says about them.

So the product is never sold as a plugin. It is sold as a **job to be done**:
*be discoverable and correctly described when a buyer — or their agent — asks
an AI about your category.*

Two framings that work, in this order:

- **Risk.** "Right now an AI describing your category can name a competitor and
  not you, or describe your offer wrongly. You cannot see it happening."
- **Upside.** "Your site becomes a machine-readable micro-database, so AI can
  describe you correctly and route high-intent buyers to the right page."

The demo does the selling: run the free audit on their own domain, live.

the vocabulary.

## 4. THE FUNNEL

```
book / LinkedIn / partner  →  free audit on 3webobs  →  checklist with gaps
     →  free L1 injection (lead magnet, edge-only, no site changes)
     →  paid L3+ (SSOT, entities, intents)  →  monitoring subscription
     →  content layer (separate engagement, separate price)
```

L1 is given away deliberately. It costs nothing to serve, it is publicly
verifiable, and it puts our Edge in the path — which is what makes L3+ a
one-click upgrade instead of a new project.

## 5. WHAT COMPETITORS SELL AND WHY WE ARE NOT THAT

- **Edge SEO platforms** (SearchPilot and similar) sell A/B testing at scale
  for large SEO teams. We sell semantic legibility plus governance plus an
  ON/OFF switch, for firms with no SEO team.
- **AI visibility trackers** sell dashboards that measure mentions. Measuring is
  not fixing. We fix, and let an independent auditor measure.
- **Schema plugins** sell markup inside the CMS. That is exactly the risk we
  remove: themes and plugins already inject schema, and a second copy creates
  duplicates and contradictions that survive every update.

Our differentiator, in one line for a deck:
**"They test what moves Google. We make the offer legible to AI and controllable
like a feature flag — with proof anyone can check in 60 seconds."**

## 6. TIMING ARGUMENTS THAT ARE HONEST

- AI Act transparency obligations are in force; a machine-readable declaration
  is cheap insurance and a differentiator.
- Paid AI placement is arriving; organic legibility is what you own.
- Crawl-control and pay-per-crawl schemes are appearing at the CDN layer, which
  makes the allow-lane a real decision rather than a default.

## 7. WHAT WE NEVER PROMISE (repeat, because sales forgets)

Citations. Rankings. ChatGPT Browse visibility. Traffic numbers. Legal
certification. Compliance without a client sign-off.
