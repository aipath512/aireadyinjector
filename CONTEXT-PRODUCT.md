## 2. THE THREE WEBS

| Web | Who reads | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| Human Web | people, browsers, Google | pages, speed, clarity, trust |
| AI Web | ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews | machine-readable structure, entity identity, intent maps |
| Machine Web (A2A) | autonomous agents | agent cards, capability contracts, invocable endpoints |

A business can rank on Google and be completely absent from the other two.
That gap is the product.

## 3. THE LOOP (this is the whole product)

```
  URL in
```

The client does not buy a report. They buy the loop staying on.

## 4. WHAT WE INJECT VS WHAT WE DO NOT

**Automatic (no approval needed)**
metadata, JSON-LD, internal link hints, AI discovery files
(`robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, `ai.txt`, `ai.json`), entity graph, intent maps,
agent card / A2A endpoints, response headers, crawler allow-lane.

**With client approval**
commercial copy, prices, policies, compliance declarations, `sameAs` links,
organization schema (needs name, address, founding date).

**Monitored only — never claimed as delivered**
reviews, backlinks, press, Wikipedia, Crunchbase, rankings, AI citations,
E-E-A-T author authority, topical depth.

**The boundary sentence, verbatim, use it in sales:**
> The injector makes your site legible to AI. It does not make it credible.
> Legibility is installed. Credibility is earned.

## 5. WHY EDGE, NOT CMS

- No CMS access needed, no plugin, no developer, no downtime.
- Live worldwide the moment it deploys; rollback is one write.
- Works identically on WordPress, static, Webflow, Shopify, custom.
- The site's design, theme and content are never touched.

The honest limit: this works when the client's traffic passes through our Edge.
See `/specs/INJECTOR-ENGINE.md` §6 for the three onboarding paths and what each
one costs the client in DNS terms.

## 6. MATURITY LEVELS (client-facing language)

- **L0** — normal site. AI may or may not understand it.
- **L1** — AI entry files live at the Edge (`robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, `ai.txt`),
  crawler allow-lane open, sitemap reachable. No site changes.
- **L2** — `ai.json` manifest served from a single source of truth (KV), so
  updates are instant with no redeploy. Publicly verifiable by response headers.
- **L3** — intent library (`intents.json`) routes user needs to the right page.
- **L4** — declared actions (`actions.json`): agents can do, not just read.
- **L5–L6** — controlled automation, memory, measured AI visibility.

The injector's paid floor is L2. L1 can be given free as the lead magnet.
