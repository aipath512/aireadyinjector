# /context/OBJECTIONS.md — What They Say Back
Repo: aipath512/aireadyinjector · Session: 0001C · v1.0 · 2026-09-04

Answers must be honest and short. If an answer needs a promise we cannot keep,
the answer is "no" and we lose the deal. That is acceptable.

---

**"My site is already online. Why would I pay for this?"**
Online means humans can read it. AI systems read a different layer — policy
files, entity declarations, intent maps, machine endpoints. Most sites publish
none of it. Run the free audit on your domain and look at the AI and Machine
columns.

**"Will this get me into ChatGPT?"**
No. Nobody can promise that, and anyone who does is selling you something they
do not control. This makes you legible. Being cited is earned through content
and external corroboration. Two different products, two different timelines.

**"So what do I actually get?"**
A publicly verifiable machine-readable layer on your own domain, kept correct
automatically, plus an independent before/after score. You can verify it
yourself in a browser in 60 seconds without access to our account.

**"My developer / agency can do this."**
They can. Three questions: will they keep it correct after every theme update,
will they detect drift hourly, and will an independent auditor score it? Most
one-off implementations decay within a quarter.

**"I don't want anyone touching my site."**
Nobody touches it. No plugin, no CMS access, no code in your theme, no
downtime. The signals are served at the network edge from a separate store.
Switching it off is one action and your site is exactly as it was.

**"What if it breaks something?"**
Human traffic passes through untouched — we only serve a fixed list of policy
paths. On the levels that do modify page markup, rollout is 10% canary first
and rollback is one click. Everything is versioned in a changelog.

**"Why do I need a DNS change?"**
Because signals are served at the edge, traffic has to reach our edge. Three
options: move nameservers, keep your own Cloudflare and we deploy into it, or a
fallback with one CNAME. The fallback delivers less, and we tell you exactly how
much less rather than selling you the full level.

**"167 signals? That sounds inflated."**
It is 167 audited criteria, of which 140 can be tested directly. Far fewer are
injectable. Reviews, backlinks, press and AI recommendations are not ours to
install — we only monitor those. The report marks every row honestly.

**"Isn't this just SEO?"**
SEO optimises for one ranking system that reads pages. This publishes structured
truth for many systems that read data, and for agents that call endpoints rather
than reading pages at all. They overlap, they are not the same job.

**"Can you guarantee compliance with the AI Act?"**
No. We are not a law firm and we never certify. We publish the technical
transparency artifacts — a declaration you sign off, machine-readable, dated and
hashed. Your lawyer decides whether your obligations are met.

**"Too expensive."**
Compare it to the alternative you were quoted: rebuilding the site. This is the
same outcome for the AI layer without redesign, without CMS migration, without
developer time. And the audit that proves the gap is free.

**"Let me think about it."**
Fine. The audit is free and takes a minute. Take the report with you — even if
you never buy, you now know what AI sees. Send it to whoever built your site.
